# LLMs & Computational Text Analysis for Political Discourse

Methodological work on integrating and validating large language models and embedding models (e.g., text-embedding-3-large, UmBERTo) in the loop for classifying and annotating political and news content at scale, including cross-national collaborative validation exercises.
