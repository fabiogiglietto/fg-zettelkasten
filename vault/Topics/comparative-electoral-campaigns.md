# Comparative Electoral Campaign Analysis

Cross-national comparative studies of social media dynamics during election campaigns, including coordinated behavior and political advertising in the 2021 German federal election, Brazilian post-Bolsonaro digital battlefields, and Italian general elections from 2018 through 2022.
