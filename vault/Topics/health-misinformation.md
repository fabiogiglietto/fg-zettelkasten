# Health Misinformation

Research on the circulation of problematic health information and coordinated networks spreading health-related disinformation, including COVID-19 fact-checking and health content studies in India and Nigeria, plus work on online counter-publics and trust in medicine. A sustained applied topic within the MINE programme's health-information strand.
