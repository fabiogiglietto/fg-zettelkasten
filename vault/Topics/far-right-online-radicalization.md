# Online Radicalization and Far-Right Mainstreaming

Studies examining how digital platforms facilitate radicalization, the normalization of far-right and conspiratorial worldviews, and the construction of oppositional identities online. The papers share a focus on how platform affordances, affect, humor, and symbolic boundary work amplify extremist or anti-institutional content.
