# Political Communication & Elections

Empirical studies of social media dynamics around national elections (Italy 2018/2022, Germany 2021, Brazil, Latin America), covering partisan news circulation, political advertising and campaign engagement. The long-running MINE programme's founding focus and a recurring source of sub-projects and publications.
