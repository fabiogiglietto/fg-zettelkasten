# Political Communication & Elections

Studies of social media dynamics around electoral campaigns (Italian 2018/2019/2022, German 2021, Brazilian post-Bolsonaro), including partisan attention, affective polarization, insularity, and cross-partisan news engagement. A long-standing empirical anchor of the MINE program that continues to generate active output.
