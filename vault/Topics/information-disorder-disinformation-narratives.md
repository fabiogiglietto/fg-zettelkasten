# Information Disorder & Disinformation Narratives

Study of fake news, misinformation taxonomies, and cross-national disinformation narrative monitoring (e.g. war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ rights, elections), anchored by the EU-funded PROMPT project and the hybrid-media 'fake news' research program.
