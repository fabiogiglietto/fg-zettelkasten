# Platform Data Access & Governance

Research on the shifting landscape of social media research APIs and data access post-CrowdTangle shutdown, the effects of the Digital Services Act and EU algorithmic transparency obligations, and scrutiny of platform content-moderation policies such as Meta's political content reduction policy.
