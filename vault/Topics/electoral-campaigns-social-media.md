# Electoral Campaigns on Social Media

Empirical mapping of national election campaigns (Italy 2018/2022, Germany 2021, European elections, Brazil post-Bolsonaro) as they unfold on Facebook, Instagram and Twitter/X, including coordinated networks, political advertising and citizen engagement. The founding empirical programme (MINE) and its longest-running sub-projects.
