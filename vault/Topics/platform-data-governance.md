# Platform Data Governance & Research Access

Study of social media platforms' data-access regimes and content-moderation policies for researchers and fact-checkers — including the Digital Services Act, Meta's URL Shares Dataset thresholds, the CrowdTangle shutdown, Meta's political content reduction policy, and service as a JRC/ECAT expert under DSA Art. 40(12). Central to the researcher's current methodological and policy-facing agenda.
