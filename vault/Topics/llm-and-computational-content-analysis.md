Methodological research into using large language models and embedding techniques for text classification, annotation, and clustering in communication studies, including validation challenges of LLM-in-the-loop pipelines — a fast-growing methodological front tied to current teaching (Generative AI and Media) and multiple 2024-2026 publications.
