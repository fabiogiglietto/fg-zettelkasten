# Electoral Communication & Cross-National Election Studies

Empirical studies of political communication and coordinated online activity around specific elections — Italian (2018, 2022), German (2021), and Brazilian (post-Bolsonaro) — a durable comparative research line spanning the MINE programme's country-focused sub-projects.
