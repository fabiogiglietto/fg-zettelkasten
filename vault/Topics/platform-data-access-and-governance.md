# Platform Data Access & Governance

Research on the changing landscape of social media research APIs, data access tools, and platform governance under the Digital Service Act, including analyses of Meta's policy shifts (URL Shares Dataset thresholds, Political Content Reduction Policy) and the transition from open ('Wild West') to restricted ('Walled Garden') data environments.
