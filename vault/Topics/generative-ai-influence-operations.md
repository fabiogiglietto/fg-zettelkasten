# Generative AI & Influence Operations

Emerging focus on how generative AI is used in coordinated persuasion and deceptive campaigns (e.g. synthetic visuals in gambling promotion) and on employing LLMs as research tools for classification and annotation of political discourse. Reflects a new active teaching module (IA Generativa e Media) and a growing 2025-2026 publication stream.
