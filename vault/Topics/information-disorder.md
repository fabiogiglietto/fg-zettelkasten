# Information Disorder & Disinformation Narratives

Study of disinformation, misinformation, and 'fake news' as phenomena within the hybrid media system, including monitoring of disinformation narratives (war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ rights, elections) under the PROMPT project and foundational taxonomy work on misleading information.
