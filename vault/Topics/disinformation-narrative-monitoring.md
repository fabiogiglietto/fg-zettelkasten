# Disinformation Narrative Monitoring

Monitoring and characterizing disinformation narratives circulating across Europe (war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ rights, elections) and the development of dashboards and resources for fact-checkers and journalists, centered on the PROMPT and MINE-FACTS projects and related information-operations case studies.
