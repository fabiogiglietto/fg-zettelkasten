# Disinformation Narrative Monitoring

Tracking and modeling the spread of disinformation narratives across Europe (e.g. Ukraine war, LGBTQI+ rights, elections) using language models and monitoring dashboards, as pursued through the PROMPT project and related fact-checker-facing tools.
