# Elections & Social Media Campaigns

Longitudinal research programme (MINE) mapping Italian and cross-national election campaigns—2018, 2019, 2022 Italian elections, 2021 German federal election—tracking news coverage, coordinated activity and citizen engagement around electoral processes.
