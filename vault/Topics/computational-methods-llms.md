# Computational Methods & LLMs for Content Analysis

Methodological work on integrating large language models and embedding models into political discourse and communication research pipelines, including validation of LLMs-in-the-loop annotation, human-machine disagreement, and comparative embedding evaluation for clustering political news. A rapidly growing, current strand tied to the researcher's PhD/Master teaching in generative AI.
