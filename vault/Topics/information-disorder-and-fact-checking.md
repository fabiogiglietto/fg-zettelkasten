# Information Disorder & Fact-Checking

Conceptual and empirical work on misinformation, disinformation and 'fake news' within the hybrid media system, including taxonomies of misleading information and tools to speed up fact-checking (e.g. MINE-FACTS, PROMPT). A foundational, career-spanning theme explicitly listed among the researcher's core interests.
