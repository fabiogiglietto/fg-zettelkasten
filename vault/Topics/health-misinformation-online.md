# Health Misinformation & Digital Gatekeeping

Research on the circulation of problematic health content and its drivers on social media in cross-national contexts (India, Nigeria, COVID-19 Italy), and on the gatekeeping role of platforms like Google News in shaping health information exposure. Connects earlier Gates Foundation-funded work to ongoing interest in health-related information ecosystems.
