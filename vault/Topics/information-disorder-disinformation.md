# Information Disorder & Disinformation

Broad study of the spread, taxonomy and mitigation of fake news, disinformation narratives and fact-checking infrastructures, including the PROMPT and MINE-FACTS projects and foundational hybrid-media-system analyses of misleading information. Remains a core conceptual umbrella linking the researcher's applied and theoretical work.
