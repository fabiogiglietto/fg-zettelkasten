# Platform Data Access and Research Infrastructure

Critical examination of social media research APIs, data access regimes, and platform governance shaping academic research, including the transition from CrowdTangle to Meta's Content Library and DSA-era access tools. Connects to the vera.ai/PROMPT toolbuilding work and recent state-of-the-field reports.
