# Comparative Digital Media and Elections

Cross-national comparative studies of coordinated political advertising, information campaigns and platform dynamics around elections beyond Italy (Germany, Brazil, Latin America, Nigeria), extending the researcher's coordination-detection methods to new electoral and political contexts.
