# Political Polarization & Partisan Engagement

Study of affective and structural polarization, partisan community dynamics, and algorithmic/media amplification of extremes, connecting historical work on media insularity with recent longitudinal analyses of partisan Facebook networks (e.g., pro-Bolsonaro communities).
