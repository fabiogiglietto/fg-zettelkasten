# Political Polarization & Partisanship

Analysis of affective and structural polarization dynamics across social and traditional media, including partisan community engagement (e.g., pro-Bolsonaro networks in Brazil), cross-national comparisons in Latin America, and the interplay between social and legacy media in amplifying political extremes.
