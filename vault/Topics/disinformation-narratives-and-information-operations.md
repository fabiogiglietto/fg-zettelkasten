# Disinformation Narratives & Information Operations

Monitoring and mapping the propagation of disinformation narratives (war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ issues, European elections) and deceptive information operations, including generative-AI-driven persuasion tactics (e.g. gambling promotion, 'Zombie Internet' cases). Anchored in the PROMPT project and its associated recent publications.
