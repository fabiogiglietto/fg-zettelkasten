# Disinformation Narratives and Information Operations

Monitoring and characterizing disinformation narratives and deceptive information campaigns (e.g. war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ rights, elections, gambling promotion), including the anatomy of 'zombie' and iterative deception operations. Anchored in the PROMPT project and multiple 2025-2026 papers on strategic information operations.
