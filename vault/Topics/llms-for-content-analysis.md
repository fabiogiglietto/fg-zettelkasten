# LLMs for Political Content Analysis

Methodological work on integrating and validating large language models within human-in-the-loop pipelines for classifying and annotating political discourse and news content, including comparative embedding-model studies. A rapidly growing methodological line with multiple 2024-2025 papers and international collaborations.
