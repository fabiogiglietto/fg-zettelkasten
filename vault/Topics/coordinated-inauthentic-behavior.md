# Coordinated Inauthentic Behaviour

Detection, monitoring and theorization of coordinated link-sharing and inauthentic network activity on social platforms, from the original CooRnet/CLSB methodology to the ongoing VERA-AI Alert system and case studies (Italian elections, German elections, Nigeria, India, Ukraine, 'Zombie Internet' operations). This is the researcher's most sustained and currently active line of work, anchored in PROMPT and vera.ai WP4.
