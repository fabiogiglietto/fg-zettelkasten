# Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior

Detection, monitoring, and theorization of coordinated link-sharing and inauthentic network activity across platforms — the researcher's signature methodological line, spanning CooRnet, the VERA-AI Alert system, and election-specific network studies in Italy, Germany, and Brazil.
