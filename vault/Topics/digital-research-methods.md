# Digital Research Methods & Ethics

Methodological contributions on collecting and analyzing social media data responsibly, including Facebook ad-based survey recruitment, ethics in computational social science, and best practices for using platform APIs as research data sources. Underpins the researcher's broader empirical agenda across two decades.
