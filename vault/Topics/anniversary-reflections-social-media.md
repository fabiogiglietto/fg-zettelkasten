# Anniversary Reflections on the Trajectory of Social Media

Retrospective essays revisiting the development of social media platforms over the past decade or two, diagnosing how early promises of sociality, intimacy, and openness have given way to algorithmic curation, platform consolidation, parasocial engagement, and extractive political economies. The papers share a reflective, intervention-oriented stance written for a journal anniversary issue.
