# Cross-National Influence Operations

Comparative investigation of deceptive information operations and coordinated networks across diverse national contexts (Nigeria, India, Ukraine war narratives, Brazil), tracing how actors adapt tactics ('spaghetti-throwing' iterative deception) across platforms and regions.
