# Platform Governance & Algorithmic Content Curation

Analysis of how platform policies (e.g. Meta's political content reduction) and algorithmic governance shape visibility of political and news content, including studies of Facebook's URL Shares Dataset thresholds and the shift from open to 'walled garden' platform ecosystems.
