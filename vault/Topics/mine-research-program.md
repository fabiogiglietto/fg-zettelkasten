# MINE — Mapping Italian News Program

The institutional and intellectual umbrella coordinated by the researcher since 2017, hosting successive externally funded sub-projects on Italian media coverage, social media's role in elections, public opinion and health information. Remains the organizing structure for the whole research agenda even though individual grants have concluded.
