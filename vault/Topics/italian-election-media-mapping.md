# Italian Election Media Mapping (MINE)

The MINE programme's core empirical line mapping Italian news media and social media coverage across national election cycles (2018, 2019, 2022), studying Facebook/Instagram engagement, hyperpartisan networks and citizen interaction with electoral campaigns; the institutional umbrella for the researcher's ongoing agenda.
