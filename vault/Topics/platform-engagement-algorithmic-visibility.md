# Platform Engagement & Algorithmic Visibility

Empirical study of how algorithmic governance, partisan alignment and content quality shape visibility and engagement on Facebook and Instagram, including reaction-based engagement, affective community dynamics, and citizen interaction with institutional campaigns.
