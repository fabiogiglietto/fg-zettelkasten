# Generative AI & Media Manipulation

Investigation of how generative AI is used to produce and evolve deceptive visual and textual content in coordinated influence operations (e.g. AI-generated gambling promotion imagery), and broader concern with countering disinformation in the generative-AI era. Directly connected to the new 'IA Generativa e Media' course and the researcher's most recent publications.
