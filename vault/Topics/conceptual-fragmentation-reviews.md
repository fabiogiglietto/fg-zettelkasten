# Reviews diagnosing conceptual fragmentation in communication/media research

Systematic or integrative literature reviews that map a rapidly growing but conceptually fragmented subfield of communication/media studies (the political/non-political boundary, climate change visualization, algorithm literacy), diagnosing inconsistent conceptualizations, methodological and geographic imbalances, and calling for more integrative or computational frameworks to advance cumulative knowledge-building.
