# Generative AI and Synthetic Media Disinformation

Emerging strand examining how generative AI is used to produce and scale deceptive content (synthetic images, LLM-generated text) in information operations, and how AI tools can assist detection and countering of disinformation; reflected in recent 2025-2026 publications and the new IA Generativa e Media course.
