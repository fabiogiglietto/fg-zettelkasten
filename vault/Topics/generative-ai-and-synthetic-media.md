# Generative AI and Synthetic Media

Study of generative AI's role in disinformation, including synthetic visual persuasion, AI-generated content in coordinated campaigns, and the broader transformation of the disinformation landscape by generative technologies. Reflects current teaching (IA Generativa e Media) and recent book chapters/publications.
